2021

*tools: [[Unity]], [[OpenXR]]

A VR app for intuitively creating structures using shapes and prefabs. 
![](https://youtu.be/TQhPNjC5124)

The user's off-hand acts as the material palette / settings screen where you can modify the color of light sources and the behavior of particle effects. The main hand brush can create, scale and move objects. To fully take advantage of VR, users can crouch to shrink and view the model in real-life size (shown at 2:47 in the video).


Selected for the U-22 Award at [[25th JAPAN MEDIA ARTS FESTIVAL]].
