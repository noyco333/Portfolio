*tools: [[Unity]], [[Cluster]]*

A third-person beat-em-up action game made for Cluster. The goal was to let players experience simple yet flashy and stylish combat in their favorite avatars.
There is only one attack button, but the moves change depending on the players movement (stationary -> normal attack, running -> lunge attack, jumping -> aerial swing that acts as a double jump, falling -> ground slam). There are both melee and ranged enemy variants, but all damage can be parried by pressing the attack button at the right time. Players can also wallrun on just about any surface.
