It's pretty obvious that Cluster's scripting system or netcode isn't the best for realtime combat. I had an idea to make an RPG-like experience that doesn't involve combat (at least in a conventional way). Inspired by games like Portal and Zelda TOTK, I made a telekinesis wand that lets you play around with the physics engine.
