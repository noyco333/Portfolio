October 2022: Speaker at Japan Digital Day, Conversed with Mr. Kono, Japan digital minister
November 2022: Mr. Kono’s online observation of The Fessenden School and its approach to digitizing education


In Japan, "[Digital Day](https://www.digital.go.jp/news/0af240e8-4922-4464-95d9-bf3184c25285/)" has been established as an annual opportunity for citizens to reflect on and reconsider the digitalization of the country. Last year on Digital Day, I was fortunate enough to be personally chosen by the Digital Agency to join the discussion with [Minister of Digital Transformation, Mr. Kono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taro_Kono).
