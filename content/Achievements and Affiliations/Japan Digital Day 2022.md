October 2022: Speaker at Japan Digital Day, Conversed with Mr. Kono, Japan digital minister
November 2022: Mr. Kono’s online observation of The Fessenden School and its approach to digitizing education


In Japan, "[Digital Day](https://www.digital.go.jp/news/0af240e8-4922-4464-95d9-bf3184c25285/)" has been established as an annual opportunity for citizens to reflect on and reconsider the digitalization of the country. Last year on Digital Day, I was fortunate enough to be personally chosen by the Digital Agency to join the discussion with [Minister of Digital Transformation, Mr. Kono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taro_Kono). During our discussion, I mentioned some of the digitization efforts at The Fessenden School as an example of ICT integration in education. The next month, Mr. Kono decided to conduct an online observation of The Fessenden School. It was somewhat challenging to coordinate this event involving the school, but I was able to lead and successfully organize the tour. I was very pleased to briefly serve as an ambassador of education tech between Japan and the United States, and I hope it leads to advancements in the future. I would like to continue introducing the technological feats of American education to Japan. I hope my proficiency can aid the progression of education in Japan, even if it is marginal.
