2022: 25th JAPAN MEDIA ARTS FESTIVAL U-18 Award

  

The [Japan Media Arts Festival](https://j-mediaarts.jp/en/) is a media arts event organized by the Agency for Cultural Affairs of Japan, which honors top project submissions in four categories: Art, Entertainment, Animation, and Manga. They also provide an opportunity for guests to enjoy the award-winning pieces. The 25th annual festival received submissions from over 95 countries worldwide, with more than 3,500 entries. My VR work, VR Sandbox, was chosen as [the sole winner of the Under-18 award](https://j-mediaarts.jp/en/award/single/vr-sandbox/).
