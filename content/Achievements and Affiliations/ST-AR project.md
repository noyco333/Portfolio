June 2026: Admitted

ST-AR project (STudent Academia Research project) is an organization that provides opportunities for high school students to experience the daily life of a researcher through  
"research internships." I applied to this program to see if I want to research at college and beyond.
![[IMG_2160.png]]

I was granted to stay for a few days at University of Tokyo's Information Somatics lab (A.K.A. Inami Lab), one of Japan's most advanced XR research labs. I cooperated with the two other ST-AR project member to plan and execute a research project about giving people wings in VR as a method of locomotion.
