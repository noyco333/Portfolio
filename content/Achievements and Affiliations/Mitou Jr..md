2021: Mitou Jr. Outstanding Performance Award

  

The [Mitou Jr.](https://jr.mitou.org/english/) program (Mitou meaning “Unexplored”) is an organization hosted by an external organization of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry. It aims to support individuals with exceptional technical skills under the age of 17 who are exploring innovative ideas.

  

To apply, I developed [[VR SANDBOX]], an easier, more intuitive 3D modeling app compared to modern to flatscreen alternatives in a VR environment. Users can generate meshes by using their hand controllers like a brush and palette, eliminating the need to combat unintuitive UI, keybinds or perspective. At the end of the year-long term, I received the [Outstanding Performance Award](https://jr.mitou.org/english/stats).

  

As of 2023, I still participate in the Mitou Jr. community as an alum, contributing to the development of younger members. I feel that Mitou Jr. helps create bonds between individuals in the IT field in Japan.
