December 2022: Visionary Talk Speaker 
December 2022: U25 sector Best Project Award

[XR Kaigi](https://www.xrkaigi.com/) (Kaigi meaning “conference”) is an annual Japanese conference between the leaders of VR, AR, MR and metaverse development. [I was invited to be a speaker at this event](https://youtu.be/0IxqQTR8Zy0?t=1257) after the summer VR exhibition I hosted in the summer of 2021. This led me to submit my intuitive VR 3D modeling software to the closely related competition, later commemorated as [the best project award in the U25 sector.](https://youtu.be/_EKL2etG3xg?t=1427)
