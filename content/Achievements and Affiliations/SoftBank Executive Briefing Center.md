September 2022 and ongoing: Hosted VR exhibition


[SoftBank](https://www.softbank.jp/en/corp/aboutus/), one of Japan’s leading companies, is a company founded by Masayoshi Son, currently focusing on domestic and international cellular networking services. [SoftBank’s Executive Briefing Center](https://www.softbank.jp/biz/ebc/) provides a space for business customers to discuss their business growth through live demonstrations of the leading use cases for 5G, AI and IOT. There are many devices prepared to help customers visualize their specific path to the future.

  

Since September 2022, my procedural terrain generating VR software, [[Drift Abyss]], has been offered a permanent exhibition plot in the Executive Briefing Center. I am grateful that I can contribute to the visitor’s ideas and ambitions through my project.
